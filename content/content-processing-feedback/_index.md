Content processing feedback pages document the changes I make to the content processing done by Content Freelancer(s), week by week. Content processing is rather complex overall, and I decided that rather than trying to DM Content Freelancers feedback about content processing, it is better to build a backlog of feedback, to serve as a growing body of guidance on how exactly I want the processing to proceed, and why.

To keep scope manageable, I've decide to just make one feedback page per year.
