### [Group discussion: Assurance of salvation](https://www.bibledocs.org/outline-content/what-is-the-bare-minimum-required-to-be-saved/#group-discussion-assurance-of-salvation)

#### Content thumbnail description

Initial value:

- "Image of a page of the Bible with a salvation passage, with a text overlay of 'am I saved?'"

Changed to:

- "Image/clip-art/etc. of a nervous/anxious person, with a text overlay of 'am I saved?'"

Why:

- Thumbnails will not be able to display the detail necessary to render specific Bible passages, nor is it realistic to find images to use in such a way. So this sort of thing will never work as thumbnail backgrounds (not just in this case specifically, but ever).
